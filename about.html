About this paper

The Frontier Gazeta is a twice-weekly paper built from long-form podcasts and video. Every Wednesday and Sunday at 8am Eastern it covers each episode the 30 shows below published since the last edition, across AI, venture, crypto, markets and macro.

Each episode gets a short summary and five takeaways, written around why they matter. Episodes run in order of how much new information they carry. The opening essay, The Thread, covers what the shows were arguing about and where independent voices landed in the same place.

The summaries are machine-written from the episodes themselves, under strict rules about sourcing, and edited by Gerard Barile. Quotes and figures come from the episodes. Every entry links to the source, so you can check anything that matters to you.
